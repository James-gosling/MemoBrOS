---
name: Summarize attachment
description: The user attached a PDF or document and wants to know what it says, or asks a question about it.
---

Read the attached file and answer out loud in at most four sentences:

1. What the document is (type, who it's from, date if shown).
2. The two or three points that matter most to the reader.
3. Anything that needs action from them, with deadlines or amounts stated exactly as written.

Quote numbers and names exactly. If the question can't be answered from the file, say so plainly instead of guessing. If what's on screen is related to the document (a form to fill, an app it describes), point at the relevant place.
