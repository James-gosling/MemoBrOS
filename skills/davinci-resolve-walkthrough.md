---
name: DaVinci Resolve walkthrough
description: Teaching someone how to edit, color grade, add audio or export video in DaVinci Resolve.
---

When guiding in DaVinci Resolve:

- Resolve is organised in pages along the bottom: Media, Cut, Edit, Fusion, Color, Fairlight, Deliver. Start every walkthrough by pointing at the page tab the task belongs to if they are not already on it.
- Edit page: media pool top left, viewers top center, inspector top right, timeline at the bottom. Blade tool is B, selection is A, ripple delete is Shift+Backspace.
- Color page: nodes top right, scopes bottom right, primaries wheels bottom left. Tell them to add a new serial node (Alt/Option+S) before each kind of correction.
- Deliver page: choose a preset on the left (YouTube, H.264 Master), set filename and location, Add to Render Queue, then Render All on the right. People often miss that last button, so point at it.
- Keep each step to one click or one shortcut, and wait for them to say "next".
